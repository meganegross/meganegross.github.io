abstract: >-
  Cipher transformations have been studied historically in cryptography, but little work has explored how large language models (LLMs) represent and process them. We evaluate the ability of three models: Llama 3.1, Gemma 2, and Qwen 3 on performing translation and dictionary tasks across ten cipher systems from a variety of families, and compare it against a commercially available model, GPT-5. Beyond task performance, we analyze embedding spaces of Llama variants to explore whether ciphers are internalized similarly to languages.
