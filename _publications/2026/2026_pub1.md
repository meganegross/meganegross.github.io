pub_date:       "2026"
abstract: >-
  This paper evaluates the accessibility fairness of Generative AI for PWDs and investigates how current AI technology may neglect to address specific problems they face. Through automatic testing of two Generative AI models, OpenAI’s ChatGPT and Google’s Gemini, the usability of each model is explored. Comparisons are made between standalone technology like ChatGPT and integrated technology like Gemini in Gmail.
